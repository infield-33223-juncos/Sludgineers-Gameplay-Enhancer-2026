## 🎮 About the Game

Sludgineers is a casual single-player simulation and active incremental game developed by Snickerdoodle Games and Funk Games. It released on August 25, 2026. Players clean, mine, and refine pollution while traveling through the countryside, building a refinery, processing materials, upgrading equipment, unlocking areas, and completing quests.

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sludgineers**. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement and countryside travel remain controlled by the game. The trainer focuses on progression systems rather than changing navigation or map controls. Use movement normally to reach pollution sites, resource areas, refinery locations, and newly unlocked regions.

### Tools & Inventory

Sludgineers centers on collecting pollution and materials for processing rather than traditional combat inventory management. Activate the cash or resource options before purchasing upgrades, processing materials, or meeting quest requirements. Disable resource options before making important purchases if you want to preserve normal progression behavior.

### World & Environment

Travel through the countryside, clean pollution, mine resources, complete quests, and unlock progressively larger and more complex areas. Fast cleaning, unlimited resources, and instant refinery processing can shorten the time needed to develop the refinery and access later content.

### Combat & Weapons

Sludgineers does not use a conventional combat system. Progression is based on pollution cleaning, mining, refinery processing, equipment upgrades, power-ups, quests, and automation. Combat-related trainer options are therefore not included.
